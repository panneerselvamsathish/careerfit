careerfit

Compare a resume with a job description. Every result quotes the line it relies on.

Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version

  • Your resume is read on careerfit's server to produce the result, then deleted. It is never kept.
  • careerfit counts visits with a random ID that only your browser holds. You can turn that off below.
  • Your email and a job description's text are kept only if you choose to give them.
  • No cookies, no advertising, no third-party analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

Your files

careerfit runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS Lambda, US East, Ohio). When you analyse, your resume and the job description are written to a temporary folder on that server, read by careerfit's keyword rules, and deleted before the result is sent back. The web app never sends them to an AI model or anyone else. Only a job description you choose to donate is kept (below); your resume never is.

Your choices

careerfit doesn't show a consent banner: the first visit is counted. To stop being counted in this browser, turn usage counts off: no more usage events are sent, your analyses are left out of the skill statistics, and the random ID is deleted.

Who runs careerfit

careerfit is an independent, open-source project. The code, including exactly what is recorded (usage.py), is public at github.com/panneerselvamsathish/careerfit. Questions can go to a GitHub issue; don't post personal details there. If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
