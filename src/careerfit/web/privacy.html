Compare a resume with a job description. Every result quotes the line it relies on.

Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026

The short version

  • Your resume is read on careerfit's server to produce the result, then deleted. It is never kept.
  • careerfit counts visits with a random ID that only your browser holds. You can turn that off below.
  • Your email and a job description's text are kept only if you choose to give them.
  • No cookies, no advertising, no third-party analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

Your files

careerfit runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS Lambda, US East, Ohio). When you analyse, your resume and the job description are written to a temporary folder on that server, read by careerfit's keyword rules, and deleted before the result is sent back. The web app never sends them to an AI model or anyone else. Only a job description you choose to donate is kept (below); your resume never is.

What careerfit keeps

WhatWhenWhat it holdsKept for
Usage counts On every visit, unless you turn them off A random ID your browser made (none if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track), what happened (a page view, an analysis with its counts of matched, gap and ambiguous skills, a download, a filter, a quote opened, a waitlist sign-up), the name of the site that linked to careerfit and any campaign tags in that link, and the day. No IP address, no time of day, no file names or document text. About 13 months
Skill statistics Each analysis, unless you turn usage counts off The names of the skills from careerfit's own list that the job description asked for, whether each was a must or nice to have, whether it was matched, a gap or ambiguous, and the day. Not linked to your ID or to you. About 13 months
Waitlist email Only if you submit it Your email address, the day you joined, and what you agreed to: news of careerfit's AI analysis. Never shared. Until you remove it
Donated job description Only if you tick the box The job description's text with email addresses and phone numbers removed (a best effort, so don't donate one with other personal details), its file type, and the day. Not linked to you, and never your resume. Kept to grow careerfit's skill list
Server logs Every request Your IP address, the time, the page or API address requested, and the response status, recorded by AWS. They are used to run the service and spot abuse. The limit of analyses per minute is counted per address in memory and not stored. 14 days

Your choices

careerfit doesn't show a consent banner: the first visit is counted. To stop being counted in this browser, turn usage counts off: no more usage events are sent, your analyses are left out of the skill statistics, and the random ID is deleted.
